eink

I get asked often why I choose to use Eink devices such as the boox go10.3 and hisense touch a9 instead of using a smartphone or smart tablet. Over the last year or 2 I have developed a stronger dislike towards my devices; going to read a book on your smartphone can very well lead you to "quickly" checking social media, and from there it can be a downward spiral. In a world that demands so much of our attention, I aim to be more intentional with my time. Using the hisense touch a9 has been helpful in reaching that goal. I avoid the potential distraction of scrolling through something that doesn't always provide useful content. Eink screens are designed to look like paper, so reading for longer periods of time is much easier than, say, reading on your phone. At night, you avoid the negative effects of using a device; it's easy on the eyes and provides other useful resources to switch pages.
